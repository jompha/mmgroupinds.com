Our Legacy

A Quarter Century of
Knitwear Mastery

Founded in 2001, M.M. Group has grown from a single factory into one of Bangladesh's most respected vertically integrated knit composite operations.

With 15,23,000 sqft of manufacturing space, over 10,000 skilled employees, and state-of-the-art machinery across knitting, dyeing, cutting, sewing, printing, embroidery, and washing — we offer true end-to-end garment manufacturing under one roof.

  • 100% Export Oriented Manufacturer
  • OEKO-TEX, WRAP & BSCI Certified
  • Serving 15+ Global Fashion Brands
  • $120M+ Annual Turnover
