Sustainability Initiatives

GHG Emission Reduction Plan

We have set a plan to reduce 14% of GHG emissions by the year 2029 through various initiatives:

  • Solar panel capacity: 1,160 KW
  • Using daylight and LED lights (Reduce 0.50%)
  • Exhausted Gas Boiler (Reduce 3.00%)
  • Reuse & Recycle (Reduce 0.25%)
  • Sustainable product design
  • Zero waste initiatives

Corporate Social Responsibility

Committed to society and worker welfare.

Worker Welfare & Community

We are fully committed to social responsibility and worker welfare. Our comprehensive CSR programs include:

  • Medical Facilities: 2 units with 12 beds for workers
  • Child Care Center: Capacity for 100 children
  • Biological ETP: 280 m³ treatment plant
  • Fire Safety: Full fire control room and equipment
  • Security: 24/7 security personnel and systems
  • Training: Regular safety and skill development programs
