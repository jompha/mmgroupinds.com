M.M. Knitwear Ltd.

Est. 2001 | Flagship Company

M.M. Knitwear Ltd. is the flagship company of M.M. Group, established on 1st September 2001. It is a 100% export-oriented, vertically integrated knit composite factory with end-to-end manufacturing capabilities.

With 116 production lines, 3,850 sewing machines, and a workforce of thousands, we produce 200,000 pieces of garments daily. Our in-house facilities include knitting (45 tons/day), dyeing (45 tons/day), placement printing, all-over printing, embroidery, washing, cutting, and sampling.

Mamun Knitwear Ltd.

Est. 2014 | Sister Concern

Mamun Knitwear Ltd. was established on 5th July 2014 as a sister concern of M.M. Group. Located strategically near Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport, this facility specializes in knitting, cutting, and garment manufacturing.

The facility operates from a dedicated 7-story building with modern machinery, contributing to the group's overall capacity and maintaining the same high standards of quality and compliance.
